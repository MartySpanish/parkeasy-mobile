The questions committees actually ask

What about insurance and liability?

Drivers park at their own risk, and that's stated in our terms and shown at checkout — ParkEasy doesn't insure vehicles or the ground itself. What we do is take the payment, hold the booking and keep a record of who booked which slot and when.

Tell your own insurer before you list. Most policies are fine with occasional paid parking, but it's a five-minute call and it's your committee's call to make, not ours. We'd rather you asked and were sure.

Is it worth it for only a few spaces?

Ten spaces at £5, opened two days a week, is around £4,400 a year to the club at 85% — for tarmac that was sitting empty. Small car parks near a busy destination often do better than big ones in the middle of nowhere, because what a driver is paying for is the walk.

What if a driver overstays or parks badly?

Every booking has a start and end time and the driver's registration, so you know whose car it is. Tell us and we'll deal with the driver directly — you shouldn't be chasing anyone. We can also block a driver from booking your site again.

We don't clamp, and we don't run enforcement. If that matters to your site, say so when you get in touch and we'll be honest about whether we're a fit.

How does the money actually reach us?

The driver pays ParkEasy by card. Your share is paid out through Stripe to the club's own bank account — you'll set that up once, and it takes about ten minutes with your bank details and one committee member's ID.

How much work is this for the committee?

Setting up is one conversation and one form. After that you get an email when a booking is made, telling you the date, the time and how many cars. If your site is gated, that email is your cue to make sure it's open.

You're not staffing anything, not taking money and not marshalling cars.

Can we try it for one event first?

Yes, and honestly that's how we'd rather start. Put one matchday or one Saturday up, see how it goes, and decide after. There's nothing to cancel if you'd rather not continue.
