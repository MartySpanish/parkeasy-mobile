Delete your ParkEasy account

Applies to the website and the Android app

You can ask us to delete your ParkEasy account and the personal data linked to it at any time. This page is the official route for that request, whether you use parkeasy.uk or the ParkEasy app from Google Play.

How to request deletion

Email us from the address you signed up with, and we will delete the account within 30 days and confirm by reply. If you cannot email from that address, tell us and we will verify you another way.

Email a deletion request

Or write to parkeasyuk@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my ParkEasy account".

What gets deleted

  • Your login and profile (name, email, saved vehicle registration).
  • Your saved spots, favourites, alerts, timers and push subscriptions.
  • Any spot photos or community reports you submitted, unless you ask us to keep them public without your name.
  • Any active Premium subscription is cancelled first, so nothing is charged again.

What we have to keep, and for how long

  • Booking and payment records for bookings you made or received — kept for 6 years under UK tax and accounting law, then deleted. These are held separately from your account and are not used for anything else.
  • Host earnings reported to HMRC under the digital platform reporting rules — kept for 5 years from the end of the relevant reporting period.
  • Stripe keeps its own payment records under Stripe's privacy policy.

Just want to stop notifications or Premium?

You don't need to delete your account for that. Turn alerts off in the app under Alerts, or cancel Premium from your account settings. Contact us if you'd rather we did it for you.

Full details of how we handle personal data are in our privacy policy.
