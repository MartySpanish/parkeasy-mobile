The honest bits

How many people will actually see it?

Fewer than a billboard and more than your own social feed. ParkEasy has around 500 members and is growing — we are not going to dress that up. What is different is when they see you: at the moment they are deciding where to leave the car near you, not while they scroll.

Is it labelled as advertising?

Yes. Every card says "Featured" and looks different from a parking result. We are not going to disguise it as a recommendation — that would cost us the trust that makes the placement worth having.

What do you need from me?

A couple of photos, a line about what you do, your address and where you want the link to go. That is it. We build the page and send it to you before it goes live — nothing is published until you have seen it and you are happy.

Can I come off?

Any time, no notice, no reason needed. Send an email and it is gone that day.

Do you have parking mapped near me?

Usually. 741 spots across 31 towns, and if there is a gap near your door we will map it as part of setting you up — that happened for Aaron Quinn's barbers on the Andersonstown Road, where we had nothing until we pinned him.
