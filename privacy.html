Privacy policy

Last updated: 8 July 2026 · Version 1.0 · ICO registration ZC192696

Parkeasy Apps Ltd ("ParkEasy", "we", "us") is the data controller for personal data processed through parkeasy.uk and the ParkEasy app for Android. We are registered with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO), registration number ZC192696. This policy explains what personal data we collect, why, and your rights under the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018.

1 · Who we are and how to contact us

  • Controller: Parkeasy Apps Ltd, 17 Gransha Park, Belfast, BT11 8AT
  • Contact: parkeasyuk@gmail.com

2 · The data we collect

From Hosts: name, contact details, address, the address and details of the parking space, payout/bank details (collected and held by Stripe), earnings and transaction history, and — where required for tax reporting — date of birth and National Insurance number.

From Drivers: name and contact details (if provided), vehicle registration number, booking history, and payment card details (collected and held by Stripe). A vehicle registration is personal data because it can identify an individual.

From all users: device and usage information, and — only where you allow it — approximate or precise location to show nearby spaces on the map. Location is used on your device to sort and display results; it is not stored against your account.

Photos: if you add a photo of a parking spot, the photo is stored and may be shown to other users. Photos are used only for that purpose.

We do not intentionally collect special-category data.

3 · Why we use your data, and our lawful bases

  • To provide the service (create listings, take bookings, connect Hosts and Drivers, process payments) — performance of a contract.
  • To comply with legal obligations, including reporting Host (seller) information to HMRC under the UK's digital platform reporting rules, and keeping tax and accounting records — legal obligation.
  • To prevent fraud, ensure safety, resolve disputes and improve the Platform — legitimate interests.
  • To send marketing (only where you have opted in) — consent, which you may withdraw at any time.

4 · HMRC platform reporting

Where required by law, we collect, verify and report information about Hosts and their earnings to HM Revenue & Customs, and provide a copy of the reported information to the Host. This is a legal obligation on digital platforms that facilitate the rental of property (which includes parking spaces). We keep the associated records for the period required by those rules.

5 · Payments

Payments and payouts are handled by Stripe, which acts as an independent controller of your payment data. We do not store full card or bank details on our systems. See Stripe's privacy policy for how it processes that data.

6 · Hosting and processors

Our Platform is hosted using Vercel and our database is provided by Supabase, which act as our processors under written terms. Some processors may store or process data outside the UK; where they do, we rely on appropriate safeguards (such as the UK International Data Transfer Agreement or Addendum, or an adequacy decision).

7 · Who we share data with

  • Hosts and Drivers — the minimum information needed to complete a booking (e.g. a Driver's vehicle registration and booking time to the relevant Host).
  • Stripe — to process payments and payouts.
  • HMRC — as described in section 4.
  • Service providers — hosting, analytics, communications and fraud-prevention providers acting on our instructions.
  • Authorities or advisers — where required by law or to establish or defend legal claims.

We do not sell your personal data.

8 · How long we keep it

  • Booking, payment and accounting records: 6 years (tax and accounting requirements).
  • Platform-reporting due-diligence records: 5 years from the end of the relevant reporting period.
  • Account data: for as long as your account is active, then deleted or anonymised, subject to the retention periods above.

9 · Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, erase, restrict or object to the processing of your data, to data portability, and to withdraw consent. To exercise any right, contact us at parkeasyuk@gmail.com. To delete your account, see Delete your account. You also have the right to complain to the ICO (ico.org.uk), though we'd welcome the chance to resolve any concern first.

10 · Cookies and analytics

We use cookies necessary for the Platform to function and, with your consent, for analytics. Our analytics (Vercel Web Analytics) are cookieless and record page views and counts only — never who you are, where you parked, or what you searched for. You can change your choice any time via the cookie banner.

11 · The Android app

The ParkEasy app for Android shows the same service as parkeasy.uk inside the app. It asks for location only when you tap to use your location, and for the camera only when you choose to take a photo of a spot. You can revoke either at any time in your phone's Settings. The app does not read your contacts, messages, files or any other data on your phone.

12 · Changes

We may update this policy and will post the current version here with a revised date.
