Parking near Titanic Quarter — questions

Is parking at Titanic Belfast free?

The Titanic Belfast visitor car park is chargeable, from £2.20 for the first hour across 520 spaces. Queens Road, about a minute away on foot, is free on-street parking all day.

Where is the cheapest parking in Titanic Quarter?

The free on-street bays on Queens Road. They are limited, so they fill on a busy weekend or when there is an event at the arena nearby.

Can I park in Titanic Quarter and walk into the city centre?

It is about twenty-five minutes on foot to Belfast City Hall, measured in a straight line, so most people park in Titanic Quarter for the quarter itself rather than as a park-and-walk.
