Parking near Queen's University Belfast — questions

Is there free parking near Queen's University Belfast?

Yes, mostly by time of day rather than by place. The pay-and-display bays on University Road are chargeable Monday to Saturday 8am to 6pm and free outside those hours, and Stranmillis Road is the same from 9am. The Lisburn Road side streets are residential and free, about eleven minutes away on foot.

Where can I park at Queen's during the day?

Weekday daytime is the hard case, because the nearby on-street bays are all chargeable. The nearest all-day covered option is NCP Dublin Road at £2.00 an hour, roughly a nine-minute walk.

Can I park near Queen's on a Sunday?

Yes. The pay-and-display bays on University Road and Stranmillis Road are outside their charged hours on a Sunday, so they are free.
